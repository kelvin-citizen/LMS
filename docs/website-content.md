### Questions
**Everything parents ask us.**

Can't find what you're looking for? Our admissions office is always happy to help.
Link: Contact admissions

- **When are admissions open?** Admissions for the 2026/2027 academic year are ongoing. We also accept learners mid-year, subject to space in the class.
- **What age can my child start?** Our Creche welcomes children from age 1. We offer classes all the way from Creche and Nursery through Kindergarten, Primary and Junior High School.
- **What documents do I need to apply?** A completed admission form, a copy of your child's birth certificate, recent passport photographs and, where applicable, their last school report.
- **Is there an entrance assessment?** Yes. A short, friendly assessment helps our teachers understand your child's level so we can place and support them properly.
- **Can I visit the school before applying?** Absolutely. We encourage parents to visit, meet our staff and see our classrooms. Contact us to book a convenient time.

## 3. Programmes page (/programmes)

### Banner
Our programmes
**One journey, from first steps to graduation.**

From Creche to JHS 3, every stage is designed to meet children where they are and lift them higher.

Quick links: Creche & Nursery · Kindergarten · Primary School · Junior High School

### 01 Creche & Nursery (Ages 1 – 3)
Our Creche and Nursery give the youngest members of the Child Time family a gentle, loving introduction to school. Through songs, stories, sensory play and simple routines, children build independence, early language and the confidence to explore.

- Play-based learning · Early language & phonics · Caring attendants

What they learn [PLACEHOLDER]: Language & Communication · Number Play · Rhymes & Songs · Sensory & Creative Play · Social Skills · Outdoor Play

### 02 Kindergarten (Ages 4 – 5)
Kindergarten turns natural curiosity into a love of learning. Following the national KG curriculum, children learn to read and count through hands-on activities, develop fine motor skills, and grow in confidence as they prepare for Primary School.

- Reading readiness · Numbers & shapes · Rhymes, art & movement

What they learn [PLACEHOLDER]: Literacy & Phonics · Numeracy · Our World & Our People · Creative Arts · Physical Education · Religious & Moral Education

### 03 Primary School (Class 1 – 6)
From Class 1 to Class 6, pupils follow the Ghana Education Service standards-based curriculum, taught by qualified teachers who make lessons practical and engaging. We focus on strong fundamentals, critical thinking and good study habits.

- English, Maths & Science · ICT & French · Clubs & sports

What they learn [PLACEHOLDER]: English Language · Mathematics · Science · Computing / ICT · French · Our World & Our People · Ghanaian Language · Creative Arts · Religious & Moral Education · Physical Education

### 04 Junior High School (JHS 1 – 3)
Our JHS programme prepares learners for the BECE and beyond. Small classes, regular assessment, extra tuition and mentoring help every learner reach their potential, while leadership roles and career guidance prepare them for Senior High and life.

- Rigorous BECE preparation · Career guidance · Leadership opportunities

What they learn [PLACEHOLDER]: English Language · Mathematics · Integrated Science · Social Studies · Computing · Career Technology · French · Ghanaian Language · Creative Arts & Design · Religious & Moral Education
